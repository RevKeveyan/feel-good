## Full description

The Feel Good Challenge is a 55-day habit tracker from The Feel Good Society.

Each day you complete the Feel Good Five:
• Nourish — follow your nutrition plan
• Fuel — take your pods & powders
• Move — train for 30 minutes
• Mindset — practise for 5–10 minutes
• Hydrate — hit your water goal

Track your streak (with gentle streak protection), weekly progress photos, a 21-day mindset library, and unlock your Day 55 transformation reveal — before/after wheel, Future You letter, and vision board.

This app is a personal habit tracker. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.
